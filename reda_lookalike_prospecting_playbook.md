# Reda — Lookalike Client Prospecting Playbook

_How to find more vendors like our best clients (Facebook / Instagram / TikTok) and turn them into Reda accounts._

**Prepared:** 2026-06-30 · **Source:** Reda live order data + Meta/IG/TikTok research.

---

## 0. TL;DR (read this first)

Every one of Reda's clients runs the **same business**: one hero product, advertised on Facebook/Instagram/TikTok, ordered via WhatsApp DM, **paid on delivery (POD)**. So the fastest way to find more clients is brutally simple:

> **Find vendors advertising the same (or adjacent) products our top clients already sell, on pay-on-delivery, targeting Lagos — and pitch them with the proof that we already run those exact deliveries.**

The single best free tool for this is the **Meta Ad Library** (it shows who is *actively spending money* to drive orders right now). The product names in Section 5 are literally your prospect list. Work the loop in Section 4 every week.

### 3a. Meta Ad Library — your primary tool (free, official, public)

URL: **https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/**

Why it beats everything else: it only shows ads that are **live and being paid for**, so every result is a vendor with **active order flow that needs fulfillment today**. (It's Meta's public transparency tool — fair game for competitive research, no scraping needed.)

**How to use it:**
1. Open the Ad Library.
2. Set **Ad category = "All ads"**, **Country = Nigeria**.
3. Type a product keyword (Section 5). Hit search.
4. For each advertiser: open the ad, note the **Page name**, the **CTA** ("Send WhatsApp message" / "Send message"), the **price**, and whether **"pay on delivery"** is in the copy.
5. Click the page → **"See ad details" / page transparency** → see how long they've run ads and how many ads are active (longevity = a proven seller).

### 3b. Instagram / TikTok search (secondary)

- IG search the same keywords + hashtags: `#payondelivery #payondeliverylagos #lagosvendor #lagosbusiness #asseenontv #arabianperfumeng #slimmingtea #waisttrainerlagos`.
- TikTok: the same vendors run TikTok ads/organic; search the product name + "Nigeria".
- On any vendor profile, check the **link in bio** (wa.me / Selar / Paystack), the **highlight reels** ("delivery", "reviews"), and comments ("price?", "how to order") — high comment volume = real demand.

### 3c. Competitor-adjacency expansion (highest leverage)

- On a competitor's Ad Library page, the **same ad creative is often run by 5–10 different pages** (drop-shippers reuse winning videos). Each is a separate vendor → a separate lead.
- IG "Suggested for you" / "Similar accounts" on a known vendor surfaces more of the same.
- The vendor selling Product A this month often launches Product B next month — follow them.

### 3d. Your own network (don't ignore)

- Reda's **contractor/agent intake groups** already touch many vendors — ask current happy clients for **referrals** (offer a referral credit).
- Vendors talk to each other; one delighted Comfort-Global-style client is your best salesperson.

## 5. Qualifying checklist (score each prospect /10)

A prospect is **hot** when it looks like our existing winners. Score:

| Signal | Points | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Ad explicitly says **"Pay on Delivery" / "Pay on arrival"** | 3 | Core of our value (we collect + remit) |
| CTA = **"Send WhatsApp message"** (wa.me link) | 2 | Order funnel matches our intake model |
| Targets **Lagos / Nigeria** | 2 | Our coverage |
| Ad has been **running 3+ weeks** / multiple active ads | 2 | Proven seller, real volume — not a test |
| **Single hero product** (not a giant multi-brand store) | 1 | Fits our per-order ops; mega-stores have own logistics |

**7–10 = call now. 4–6 = nurture. <4 = skip.**

🚩 **De-prioritise:** big multi-brand marketplaces / phone-gadget stores / pharmacies with their own fleet (e.g. large perfume distributors like Lattafa-NG with 48k followers) — they're less likely to outsource per-order delivery than a lean single-product COD operator.

## 7. Outreach playbook

**Channel order:** Instagram/WhatsApp DM → if no reply in 48h, call the number on the ad → keep it human, vendor-to-vendor.

**The hook (your unfair advantage):** name the exact product and our existing volume.

**Cold DM / WhatsApp template (category-specific):**
> Hi 👋 I run **Reda Logistics** — we handle pay-on-delivery dispatch + payment collection for vendors in Lagos. We already deliver **~300 [Fire Stop Spray / Stand Again Oil / your product] orders a month** for sellers like you: same-day rider dispatch, we collect by transfer/cash, and **we remit your money the next morning** with a full report. Want me to send our rates and take the rider/cash headache off you?

**Follow-up (48h, no reply):**
> Quick one 🙏 — still happy to run a **free trial day** on your next batch of orders so you can see the dispatch + next-morning remittance before committing. Want to try it this week?

**Why this converts:** these vendors' #1 daily pain is **chasing riders and reconciling cash**. Lead with remittance speed + reporting, not "we deliver parcels."

**Proof to attach:** a (client-anonymised) screenshot of a daily remittance report / the "Delivered Update" share, and your coverage areas.

## 10. Scaling & automation (later)

- **Meta Ad Library API** exists (official) for building an automated watchlist of advertisers by keyword/country — worth it once a VA is manually working the list and you want to scale. Requires a Meta developer app + access token.
- **VA playbook:** hand a virtual assistant Sections 3–6; they spend 1–2h/day filling the tracker. This is the cheapest growth lever Reda has.
- **Avoid** paid "ad-spy"/scraper tools and any bulk-scraping of IG/FB — ToS risk and poor data quality. The Ad Library + disciplined manual work is cleaner and free.

## 11. Caveats & compliance

- Some of our best niches (body-enhancement, herbal/sexual wellness) face **ad-policy restrictions** — vendors' pages get disabled and they spin up new ones often. That's actually **a tailwind for Reda**: their ops are chaotic, so reliable dispatch + remittance is exactly what they lack. Reda is the **logistics provider, not the advertiser**, so the ad-policy risk isn't ours — but vet that a product is legal to deliver.
- Treat the starter list (Section 6) as **unverified leads** — always run the qualifying checklist before pitching.
- This is competitive research using **public** tools only; no private data, no scraping.
