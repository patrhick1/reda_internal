FAQ

Questions vendors actually ask

How do I know my money is safe?

Cash is remitted the same day it's collected. You're never waiting on us overnight. Zero missing cash on record. Start with 2 to 3 orders and watch the first run yourself.

What happens when a delivery fails?

You get the failure reason and the item comes back. Most failed attempts are not billed. A fee may apply when a rider reaches a customer who confirmed availability but then cancels, postpones or refuses payment.

Do I have to drop stock with you?

No. If you're in Lagos, our rider picks up from you per order. Stock drops are optional, or for vendors outside Lagos.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. Start with 2 orders. Grow from there if we earn it.

What areas do you cover?

~56 zones across Lagos Mainland and Island. Send your typical areas on WhatsApp and we'll confirm coverage and rates.
